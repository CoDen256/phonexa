Feature model — how the 18 overlap
Passive place — where the constriction is (chart columns)
Exo-/Endo-labial, Dental, Alveolar, Post-alveolar, Alveolo-palatal (pre-palatal), Palatal, Velar, Uvular, Pharyngeal, Epiglottal, Glottal. One value, ever — it's a location.
Active articulator — what body part moves
Labial, Apical, Laminal, Sub-apical, Antero-dorsal, Postero-dorsal, Radical. Usually implied by manner+place; stored explicitly only when it disambiguates (ʂ apical vs ʃ laminal, both post-alveolar).
Secondary articulation — multi-select overlay
Labialized, Palatalized, Velarized, Pharyngealized, Glottalized, Aspirated. Zero, one, or several at once — Russian hard л carries both Velarized and Pharyngealized simultaneously.
Everything else
Voicing (binary, per symbol) · Syllabic (binary modifier) · Co-articulation (two simultaneous primary places, e.g. w = Labial+Velar) — orthogonal to all of the above.
